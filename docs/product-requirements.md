# Confirmed product requirements

## Brand

- Business: L&G Cleaning Services
- Tagline: Quality you can see!!!

## Roles

| Role | Device | Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | iPhone | Full operations access and all client communication |
| Employee | Android | Assigned work only, job status, navigation, photos, emergency alert |

Initial employee count: 3.

## Owner app

- Manage client name, phone, address, cleaning notes, and encrypted access details.
- Create one-time and recurring appointments.
- Assign employees and view all appointments.
- Open a client address in Apple Maps.
- Create a live-ETA On Our Way SMS, review it, and explicitly approve sending it.
- Send an editable Google review request after completion.
- Receive employee start/completion and emergency notifications.

## Employee app

- View only assigned appointments.
- Open address navigation.
- Mark a job started or completed.
- Upload before and after photos; remind the employee when photos are missing.
- Cannot call or text clients through the app.
- Use a Panic button held for three seconds to create an owner alert with current location and emergency-call actions.

## Communication templates

On Our Way:

> Hi [First Name], this is L&G Cleaning Services. We're on our way and our estimated arrival is [ETA]. See you soon!

Review request:

> Thank you for choosing L&G Cleaning Services! We hope you were happy with your cleaning. We would really appreciate a quick Google review: https://g.page/r/CXzZr7XGKMfOEBM/review

## Privacy rules

- Secure access details require owner device authentication before display.
- Do not include client addresses, access codes, or detailed notes in standard notification text.
- Share an employee's location only for ETA calculation with permission or during an active panic event.
- Record job status, message sends, photo uploads, and panic event resolution in an audit log.
